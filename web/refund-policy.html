Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: November 2026

We want you to be happy with your order. This policy applies to all orders placed on trendycatch.us, shipped to Australia, New Zealand, the United States and Canada.

Change of mind

You may request a return within 30 days of receiving your item if you've simply changed your mind, provided the item is unused, in its original condition and packaging. Email orders@trendycatch.us with your order number to start a return. The cost of return shipping for change-of-mind returns is the customer's responsibility, and the original shipping charge (where applicable) is non-refundable.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

If your item arrives damaged, faulty, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery at orders@trendycatch.us with your order number and a photo of the issue. We'll arrange a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you — consistent with your consumer guarantee rights under the Australian Consumer Law, New Zealand's Consumer Guarantees Act, and equivalent protections in the US and Canada.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Stripe. Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear, depending on your bank or card provider.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene reasons, items that have been used or that are intimate/personal-care in nature cannot be returned for change-of-mind, unless faulty.

Order cancellations

If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible at orders@trendycatch.us. We can cancel and fully refund any order that hasn't yet shipped; once an order has shipped, the standard return process above applies.

Contact

For any return, refund or order issue, email orders@trendycatch.us and include your order number — we aim to respond within 1–2 business days.
