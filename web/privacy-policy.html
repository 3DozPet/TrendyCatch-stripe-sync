Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

TrendyCatch ("we", "us", "our") operates trendycatch.us (the "Site"). This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit or buy from the Site, why we collect it, and how it's handled. TrendyCatch is operated as part of 3Doz.pet. For any privacy question, contact us at orders@trendycatch.us.

Information we collect

When you place an order, our payment processor, Stripe, collects your full name, email address, phone number, billing address and shipping address directly on its own secure checkout page. We receive this information from Stripe once payment succeeds, together with the product and variant you ordered, so we can fulfil and ship it. We do not see or store your card details — those are handled entirely by Stripe.

Cookies and advertising technologies

Like most online stores, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies — including the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel, the TikTok Pixel, and Google tags (Google Analytics / Google Ads) — to understand how visitors use the Site and to measure the performance of our advertising. These tools may collect information such as the pages you view, the device and browser you use, and, where you've engaged with one of our ads, an identifier tied to that ad click (for example a Facebook, TikTok or Google click id). We also send a securely hashed (one-way encrypted) version of your email and phone number to these advertising platforms after a purchase, so they can match the sale back to the ad that led to it — this is standard "conversion tracking" used across the industry, and the platforms cannot reverse the hash back into your actual email or phone number.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of personalised advertising directly with each platform: Meta, TikTok, Google.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfil your order, including creating the order in our store system and arranging shipping.
  • To contact you about your order (confirmations, shipping updates, and customer service).
  • To measure and improve our advertising, as described above.
  • To comply with our legal and tax obligations.

Who we share it with

We share order information with the service providers who help us run the Site and fulfil orders: our payment processor (Stripe), our store/fulfilment platform (Shopify), our shipping/fulfilment partners, and — in hashed form only, for conversion tracking as described above — Meta, TikTok and Google. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain uses of it:

  • Australia — under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.
  • New Zealand — under the Privacy Act 2020.
  • United States — residents of states with applicable privacy laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA) may have additional rights regarding their personal information.
  • Canada — under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

To exercise any of these rights, email orders@trendycatch.us and we'll respond as required by applicable law.

Data retention

We retain order and customer information for as long as needed to fulfil orders, meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change.
